Hexstar 1U · CubeSat kit

Built for
near space.

A real 1U CubeSat kit for your desk, long-range field tests and stratospheric balloon flights. Flight-style boards, a LoRa radio and a pocket ground station, all in a 10 cm cube.

01 · Open it up

Every part.
Yours to see.

Four rails, four skeleton panels, a top plate with the GNSS patch and whip antenna. Everything comes apart with an M3 driver.

02 · Inside

Three boards.
One mission.

Radio on top, brain in the middle, power at the bottom. They're joined by a single 40-pin stack bus, exactly like flight hardware.
